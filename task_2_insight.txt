## Key Insights from Task 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis
Here I used cleaned dataset from task 1

1. Dataset Overview
- The cleaned dataset contains 1,200 records and 14 attributes.
- Data types are consistent and ready for analysis after cleaning.


2. Order Value Summary
- The mean order value(TotalPrice) is higher than the median, indicating the presence of a small number of high-value transactions that skew the average upward.
- Most transactions fall within a lower price range, as confirmed by the right-skewed distribution histogram.
- Boxplots for both 'TotalPrice' and 'Quantity' reveal outliers on the higher end.


3. Product Performance
- Printer was the most frequently ordered product (highest order count).
- However, Chair generated the highest total revenue — demonstrating that order frequency does not always translate to the highest revenue.


4. Payment Methods
- Online is the most commonly used payment method.
- Customers paying by Credit Card had the highest average order value, while Debit Card users had the lowest — suggesting that higher-value purchases tend to be completed using credit cards.


5. Order Status
- Order statuses are distributed relatively evenly across all categories (Delivered, Cancelled, Returned, Pending and Shipping).
- Cancelled orders are slightly higher than the other categories.


6. Referral Sources
- Instagram generates the highest number of customer referrals, making it the most effective channel.


7. Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales volume fluctuates month to month, as seen in both the monthly sales and monthly order count plots.
- These trends can be used to identify peak and low-demand periods for better inventory and marketing planning.


8. Coupon Code Analysis
- Orders without coupons had the highest return rate (24.6%).
- SAVE10 orders had the highest delivery rate (22.03%).
- WINTER15 orders had the highest cancellation rate (22.95%).
- FREESHIP generated the highest total revenue (335,036.99) and the highest average order value (1,070.41).
- Customers using WINTER15 had the lowest average order value (1,035.90).


9. Correlation Analysis
- Quantity and TotalPrice are positively correlated — more items in an order generally result in a higher total value.
- UnitPrice and TotalPrice have a strong positive relationship — higher-priced products contribute significantly to revenue.
- Quantity and UnitPrice are largely independent — customers do not necessarily buy fewer items when purchasing higher-priced products.
